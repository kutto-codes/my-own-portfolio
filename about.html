ABOUT

Building a career around secure systems and clear thinking.

I am a web security-focused learner with a practical background in systems work, scripting, and application fundamentals. My goal is to turn technical curiosity into a disciplined profession in web application security.

01

Who I am

I started by learning how websites are built, then gradually moved into understanding how they can be abused when security controls are missing or weak. That transition came naturally because I enjoy both the logic of software development and the rigour of security testing.

What I care about most is not just finding issues, but explaining them well. A vulnerability matters less if it cannot be clearly demonstrated, validated, and communicated to the people who need to fix it.
