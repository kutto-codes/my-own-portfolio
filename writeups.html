03

How I approach security

Curiosity with evidence

I like to understand how an application works before testing it. I follow evidence, question assumptions, and connect a technical observation to its practical impact.

Responsible practice

I keep my testing within an authorized scope, use practice labs, minimize data collection, and document findings in a way that supports remediation.

04

Method I follow

  • I map the application and identify relevant inputs.
  • I understand expected behavior before testing a possible weakness.
  • I validate observations with clear, appropriate evidence.
  • I document impact, risk, and reproduction steps.
  • I keep notes organized so I can review my work.
  • I check whether a proposed fix addresses the issue.
  • I prefer reproducible manual testing over blind scanning.
  • I aim to explain results clearly to technical and non-technical readers.
