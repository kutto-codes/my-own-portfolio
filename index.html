About me

I am building a career around web application security, starting from a foundation in Linux systems, scripting, networking, and how applications are built behind the scenes.

My interest lies in understanding web vulnerabilities from a practical standpoint: how inputs move through an application, where trust boundaries break, and how poor validation creates exploitable paths. I prefer focused manual testing and evidence-backed findings over shallow automation.
